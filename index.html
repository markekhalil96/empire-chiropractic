About Empire

Chiropractic care built on integrity, devotion & compassion.

At Empire Chiropractic and Rehab we offer the highest standard of patient care to ensure your physical health. Since 2011, Dr. Banh has treated patients with integrity, devotion, and compassion.

Our mission is to help you regain function and improve your overall quality of life. We are a chiropractic office that focuses on health, sports injuries, and rehabilitation for spine-related injuries — helping people live a pain-free life through chiropractic care.

Our team of experts is dedicated to providing the best care possible, offering chiropractic adjustments, massage therapy, spinal decompression, and rehabilitation exercises to help you achieve your health goals.

Auto / Personal Injury

Spinal Decompression

For a herniated or bulged disc, Non-Surgical Spinal Decompression is a non-invasive way to take pressure off the injured disc — relieving back pain and nerve conditions like sciatica.

Massage Therapy

Our skilled Licensed Massage Therapist relieves stress and reduces pain. Sports, deep tissue, and pre-natal massage offered in 60 and 90 minute sessions.

Cupping & IASTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization manipulates skin, myofascia, muscles and tendons, while cupping uses gentle suction to ease back pain, neck pain, and headaches.

Who we are

A team of caregivers dedicated to your recovery, your comfort, and your long-term wellness.

Dr. Bryan Banh

Chiropractor

Dr. Bryan Banh is a Texas native — born in Brenham, raised in Houston, and now a proud Houstonian. He attended the University of Houston and earned his Doctor of Chiropractic degree at Palmer College of Chiropractic. He has a special interest in creating partnerships with his patients. The road to recovery requires trust and hard work between doctor and patient, and Dr. Banh goes above and beyond to build that trusting relationship. He believes keeping patients proactive about recovery shortens treatment time and keeps them healthier in the future.

Nikita Morrow

Massage Therapist

Known for her signature firm pressure and ability to find the knots you didn't know you had, Nikita is dedicated to her craft. Born and raised in Houston, she began giving free back rubs to family at a young age. After undergraduate studies at Wiley College, she continued her massage education at San Jacinto College. As our longest-serving therapist, she treats every patient with patience and compassion, using myofascial trigger point release, static stretching, and more to encourage good blood flow and flexibility throughout the body.

Natalie Nguyen & Bianca Johnson

Office Manager · Clinical Assistant

Natalie is the clinic's office manager and scheduling coordinator. She brings over 15 years of experience in healthcare administration and creates a warm, happy environment so that every patient feels at home. Born in Boston, raised in St. Louis, and now a proud Texan, Natalie is a wife and a mother to a sweet 2-year-old boy. Together with our clinical assistant Bianca, they keep the clinic running smoothly and make every visit welcoming.
